Data

Privacy

  • ipScout has no server of its own. Lookups go directly from your browser to each source you enable.
  • Only the IP address (or hostname) you look up is sent. Page text that ipScout reads to find IPs stays on your device.
  • API keys are kept in this browser’s extension storage, out of reach of scripts on websites, and are sent only to the service they belong to.
  • Requests never include your cookies for these sites, so lookups aren’t tied to your logged-in accounts.
