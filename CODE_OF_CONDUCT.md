# Code of conduct

ipScout follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or publishing other people's private information.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer, [@Velidoran](https://github.com/Velidoran), through the contact options on their GitHub profile, or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue. Reports are handled confidentially.
